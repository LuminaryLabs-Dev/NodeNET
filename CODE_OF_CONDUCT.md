# Code of Conduct

NodeNET contributors are expected to keep technical discussion professional, specific, and respectful.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, doxxing, or intentionally disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Technical disagreement is welcome; focus criticism on code, architecture, evidence, and outcomes.

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project usable and safe.
